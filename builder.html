Efficient frontier — cost to reach vertical recoil

Every dot is a non-dominated build; the gold staircase is the cheapest build reaching each stat level — better is always rightward. Where the staircase goes vertical, rubles stop buying stat: that's your knee. Dashed green line is your budget. Click any dot to inspect that exact build. Frontier is beam-search approximate; prices are live (~5 min). Vertical and horizontal recoil scale by the same multiplier on every weapon (verified against all 393 in-game presets), so one recoil number ranks both — the diagram beside the selected build shows the direction your build can't change. Tarkov's "horizontal" recoil is the forward/back axis — the shove into your shoulder — not left-right sway, so it appears as its own bar rather than as screen width. Lateral wander comes from the recoil angle plus dispersion randomising it shot to shot, which is what the cone shows.
