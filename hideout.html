Your best queue — what each station should be running

Every station runs one craft at a time, so this is the most profitable recipe per station and what they earn together, restarting each craft as it finishes. Recipes that lose money are never suggested; a station with nothing profitable should sit idle.

How the numbers are made

Profit per minute = (what the output sells for − what the inputs cost) ÷ craft time. Here is how each of those three is decided.

SellingThe better of the best unlocked trader and the flea market after its listing fee. The fee is Tarkov's V₀·Ti·4P₀ + Vʀ·Tr·4Pʀ at this economy's rates (5% / 5%), cut 30% by Intelligence Center 3 and strengthened further by Hideout Management. The fee climbs steeply once you list above an item's base price, so lower list prices are tried too and whichever nets the most is used. Sometimes undercutting pays for itself.
BuyingThe cheapest trader offer your loyalty allows, filled up to its per-restock limit, then the flea for the rest. Trader offers that a task has to unlock count only while task unlocks is ticked. If every usable offer is limited, the remainder is priced at the dearest of them — buying across restocks still works, it just takes longer — and the recipe is flagged.
Inputs nobody sellsSome inputs can't be bought anywhere — the Physical Bitcoin that gets cut in half, for one. The only one you can craft with is one you already own, so it costs what you'd have got for selling it.
Thin marketsA flea price backed by fewer listings than min listings is not trusted as a sale price. When this page was built, a Vepr-to-AKM conversion topped the whole ranking at 9,000 ₽/min on the strength of a single AKM listed at 100,000 ₽, while Prapor sold the same rifle for 53,737. For inputs a thin price is still used, because it's the only price there is, and the recipe is flagged.
Craft timeBase time × (1 − 0.75% per Crafting level), down to −37.5%. Elite Crafting also runs two different recipes at once in the Workbench, Lavatory, Nutrition Unit, Medstation and Intelligence Center, and the queue fills both slots.
Bitcoin farmBase time ÷ (1 + 0.041225 × (graphics cards − 1)). Crafting skill doesn't speed it up, and the cards aren't consumed.
Tools & weaponsTools come back when the craft finishes, so they cost nothing per craft; you just need to own one. A recipe that wants a functional weapon is priced at that weapon's default preset, since a bare receiver won't do.
Left outGenerator fuel burns at the same rate whichever recipe is running, so it never changes which one wins; the queue's total is shown before it. Scav Case and Cultist Circle give random output with nothing to price. Quest crafts that need quest items are one-offs, not income.

Craft-time, fee and bitcoin formulas as tarkov.dev implements them (crafts table, flea fee, bitcoin farm); the elite Crafting perk per the wiki. Fee rates, station bonuses, graphics-card slots, recipes and prices all come live from the data.
